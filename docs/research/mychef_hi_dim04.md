[^44^]

- **Nobody owns the "wedding week" (welcome→recovery) culinary narrative except myCHEF.** Caterers sell receptions; chefs sell dinners. CJ's comes closest (welcome BBQ + reception + day-after brunch in one package). [^30^]
- **Content gap:** only Jason Raffin runs a sustained SEO blog; his domain ranks for "private chef Maui" informational queries. Wedding caterers rely on The Knot/WeddingWire/planner referrals, not first-party content.

## 4. WEDDING MARKET DEEP-DIVE

### 4.1 Market scale
- Maui (incl. Kalawao) recorded **4,659 weddings in 2021**, #2 island after Honolulu (9,943); ahead of Hawaiʻi island (2,209) and Kauaʻi (1,677). Most Hawaiʻi weddings occur May, July, October. [^49^]
- Maui is a **top-25 global destination-wedding location** (Hitched list via Radical Storage). [^79^]
- Budget benchmarks (all sourced, accessed 2026-09-06): average Maui wedding "around $25,000–$35,000 including venue, catering, photography, flowers, and planning… Beach permit weddings… $2,000–$5,000" (Maui Wedding Vendors directory) [^51^]; "Most Maui weddings cost between $3,000 and $15,000" for package-style beach/venue weddings (Simple Maui Wedding) [^54^]; pix.wedding cites average Hawaii wedding cost $40,000 / avg 85 guests [^80^]; value-planner integrated budget $12,000–$17,000 (Aloha Maui Dream Weddings) [^81^].

### 4.2 Catering economics (sourced, verbatim)
- **Maui Wedding Vendors 2026 cost breakdown:** "Heavy appetizers / cocktail $60–$90 [pp]… Buffet dinner $80–$120… Plated dinner (2–3 courses) $120–$200… Open bar (5 hrs) $40–$80… Service staff + rentals $20–$40… For a 60-guest wedding with buffet dinner and open bar, budget roughly $10,000–$16,000 for food and beverage. Resort F&B minimums are typically higher than independent caterers." [^52^]
- **Jason Raffin tiers:** "Budget tier: $50-75 per person… Mid-tier: $75-125… Premium tier: $125-200+… Hidden costs… Service charges (usually 18-22%)." [^13^]
- **Private reception dinner benchmark:** Aloha Maui Dream Weddings allocates "$4,000−$5,500" for a 15-guest private reception dinner, "a stunning $200 to $300 per-person cost, including food, non-alcoholic drinks, a simple wedding cake and gratuity." [^81^]

- **West Maui:** Ritz-Carlton Kapalua (Lava Point, Beach House Lawn, ballrooms, Cliff House) [^92^][^73^], Montage Kapalua Bay [^25^], Merriman's Kapalua [^25^], Royal Lahaina, Sheraton, Hyatt Regency, The Steeple House, Olowalu Plantation House (Olowalu, south of Lahaina — survived the fire and remains the marquee estate venue). [^82^][^25^]
- **North Shore/Upcountry:** Haiku Mill [^86^], Maui Tropical Plantation/Cafe O'Lei at the Mill House, Hui Noʻeau, Maui Pineapple Chapel, Gather on Maui. [^25^][^56^]
- **Beach ceremonies:** DLNR "Wiki permit" required for any professional-assisted beach wedding: "$0.10/sq ft, $20 minimum," 2-hour cap, **maximum ~20–25 people including vendors**, no arches/chairs/tents, liability insurance naming State of Hawaiʻi; popular permitted beaches: Makena Cove, Polo Beach, Kapalua Bay, Ironwoods. [^53^][^93^][^94^] → Structural reason receptions move off-beach to estates/villas = catering opportunity.
- **Planner ecosystem (Olowalu's hand-selected list is a good map):** Your Aloha Wedding Co., A Dream Wedding, The Perfect Wedding, A White Orchid Wedding, Opihi Love, Events by Darren Keala, A Perfect Paradise Wedding, Food for the Soul, In Bloom Maui, Exclusive Island Weddings, Makena Weddings, A Maui Wedding Day, Tropical Maui Weddings, Maui Love Weddings. [^82^] Planner referral is the dominant booking channel for estate venues; several venues **require** an approved planner (Haiku Mill, Kukahiko, Olowalu). [^86^][^69^]

### 4.4 Wedding-week pattern (multi-meal demand structure)
Documented pattern across sources: welcome dinner → rehearsal dinner → ceremony-adjacent pupus/cocktail hour → reception → recovery/farewell brunch. Evidence:
- CJ's packages explicitly bundle "welcome bbq," reception, and "day after wedding brunch." [^30^][^31^]
- Ritz-Carlton Kapalua wedding coverage: "hosting multiple events such as a welcome party, pool day, or farewell brunch… Rehearsal Dinner Options… Pre-Wedding Festivities." [^92^]
- Lotus Chefs runs "bridal weekend" + bridal brunch/high-tea services. [^3^]
- Jason Raffin: "private chef wedding services, rehearsal dinners, and post-wedding brunches." [^95^]
- myCHEF productizes exactly this: "Wedding catering Maui is a week, not a single plated hour… Welcome dinner, rehearsal, ceremony-adjacent service, reception, recovery brunch." [^44^]
**Opportunity:** no competitor sells the *week* as one culinary contract; each meal is typically re-procured from restaurants/caterers.

### 4.5 Seasonality
- Vendor-availability peaks: June–August + December holidays (book 12–18 months ahead) [^96^][^97^]; best-value shoulder: April–June and September–November (planner consensus: "September, March, or May") [^97^][^96^]; winter = whale season + wetter north shore; leeward Wailea/Kāʻanapali stay dry [^96^][^98^]; Hawaiʻi marriage peaks May/July/October. [^49^]
- Private-chef booking window: "Book 2-4 weeks ahead during peak season (December-April), 1-2 weeks during slower periods" (Raffin FAQ). [^10b^]

## 6. GAPS & OPPORTUNITIES (Maui)

1. **Own the wedding week.** $120–$200/pp plated + $40–$80 bar + multi-meal weeks are sourced norms [^52^]; no competitor contracts welcome→recovery as one product. Build per-format pages: /wedding-catering (reception), /rehearsal-dinner, /welcome-dinner, /recovery-brunch — each currently has near-zero dedicated competition.
2. **Published pricing = trust wedge.** The strongest local players (Cutting Edge, Cafe O'Lei, Tarragon, Celebrations, Food for the Soul) publish nothing; myCHEF's line-item card ($150–$250/guest, staffing hourly, 20% service, GET) beats the market's "request a quote" opacity. [^44^]
3. **Location-page land-grab.** Only Take a Chef (thin programmatic pages) and myCHEF have Wailea/Kapalua/Kāʻanapali/Lahaina pages. Dedicated pages for Wailea, Makena, Kāʻanapali, Kapalua, Kīhei, Nāpili–Honokōwai are cheap, defensible wins; treat Lahaina as a sensitive informational page routing to West Maui corridors.
4. **Cost-content SEO.** "How much does a private chef cost in Maui" is answered almost exclusively by a competitor's blog (Raffin) and platforms. A transparent, sourced cost hub (mirroring the $/pp and hourly anchors documented here) can capture the entire research-stage funnel.
5. **Estate-venue catering positioning.** DLNR beach permits cap ceremonies at ~20 people with no structures [^53^][^93^] → receptions structurally migrate to estates/villas with kitchens (Kukahiko's catering kitchen [^69^], villa chef's kitchens [^72^][^100^]). Position "estate reception catering" distinct from ballroom F&B (resort minimums "typically higher"). [^52^]
6. **Planner channel.** Estate venues require approved planners (Olowalu, Haiku Mill, Kukahiko) [^82^][^86^][^69^]; preferred-vendor lists gate access (Haiku Mill charges $650 outside-vendor fee) [^87^]. Getting onto planner/vendor lists is as important as SEO for the wedding segment.
7. **Stay Chef / chef-for-a-week whitespace.** Only Lotus ($179–$300+/pp/day) [^5^] and myCHEF (from $1,050/day) [^45^] price multi-day service; villa-rental concierges actively seek chef partners [^50^][^101^][^16^] — a partnership + landing-page play.
8. **Recovery-moment sensitivity as brand trust.** West Maui demand is back but ~16% below pre-fire [^60^]; respectful "support local" framing is both ethical and aligns with community guidance [^58^] — a tone competitors mostly ignore.
9. **Dietary/wellness depth.** Every serious Maui chef markets GF/vegan/keto competence [^1^][^15^]; retreat catering (Lotus) proves a wellness sub-market [^3^] — table stakes, not a differentiator.
10. **Review moat risk.** Raffin's "500+ five-star Google reviews" claim [^11^] sets a social-proof bar; review acquisition should be a launch KPI for the rebuild.
